## Provenance

## Proposed sequence

1. The highest space/EM-energy state is BETWEEN two masses. Space can oscillate at
   100%; the author describes this as expansion, normalized to 1.
2. Proposed positive pressure pushes space in both directions from the intervening
   region INTO the masses, where space condenses into mass.
3. Although those flows point away from the midpoint, the author does not identify
   them with a repulsive force on the masses. He proposes that the intervening space
   contracts, decreasing the separation, and identifies that contraction as gravity.
4. The proposed space/EM component decreases toward 0% at the black-hole center.
   This is not a statement that the stored mass energy or total energy is zero.
5. Electrons at the proposed center slow toward zero in a motion/oscillation variable
   that still needs a definition; energy is stored in the mass component.
6. Another incoming interaction hits the stopped configuration and restarts conversion
   into space energy and oscillation. A conserved charged state is still required;
   this is not a claim that an electron becomes a neutral photon or that light escapes
   outward through a standard GR event horizon.

## Related transport postulate

The author proposes statistical electron-to-electron energy transfer, with any electron
as a possible source or receiver, infinitely many possible paths, and a least-action
outcome. A central majority of galactic electrons is an additional proposed population
assumption. Neither receiver counts nor the standard sum over amplitudes independently
derive the proposed inward bias.

## Quantities that must remain distinct

| Label | Role in the proposal | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| 100% / 1 between masses | Normalized maximum space/EM oscillation or expansion | SI pressure, energy density, a measured local speed |
| 0% at the proposed center | Minimum space/EM component | Zero total energy or a measured black-hole interior |
| Maximum mass component | Stored/condensed energy endpoint | A known highest atomic excitation or the highest expansion state |
| Positive pressure | Proposed conversion-driving mechanism | An automatically attractive net force or a solved metric |
| Shrinking separation | Proposed gravity mechanism | Mere coordinate relabeling or independently derived proper distance |
| Internal 0° → 360° → 720° cycle | Separate illustrative electron phase bookkeeping | A derivation of spatial gradients, spin, or charge conservation |
| Infinite possible paths | Proposed transport interpretation | A completed functional integral or sequentially measured trial routes |

The table records open distinctions, not operational definitions supplied by a finished
model. Do not resolve ambiguities silently by importing a standard equation or changing
the meaning of the author's percentages.

## Constructive research gate

Specify the pressure/stress and space-to-mass conversion law, track energy and momentum,
and derive a change in a declared distance observable. A paired flow diagram is not
enough: the model must show what changes when observers measure separation and compare
that result with a matched GR/matter-field baseline. Specify the stopped variable and
compare incoming-hit and no-hit restart behavior with conservation and causal controls.
