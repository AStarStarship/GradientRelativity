## Context boundaries

Separate standard reference physics, the author's postulates, illustrative animations,
and measured or computed results. Retrieval is not validation of the author's mechanism.
Never claim a source was read from a search snippet alone.

The author's clarification is that the highest space/EM-energy state is BETWEEN masses:
100% oscillation/expansion, normalized to 1, creates proposed positive pressure pushing
space into mass. The proposed space/EM component falls toward 0% at the black-hole center;
this does not mean zero total energy. Slowing electrons, mass storage, and an incoming
impact restarting space oscillation are proposed dynamics, not a solved interior.

The author further distinguishes outward-from-midpoint flow INTO both masses from a
repulsive force: condensation is proposed to contract the intervening space and reduce
the separation, which he identifies as gravity. A shrinking drawing is not a derivation
of physical distance; pressure, conversion, stress-energy, and measurable geometry still
need a quantitative relationship.
