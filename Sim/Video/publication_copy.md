## YouTube description

Gradient Relativity proposes that mass is condensed space and that gravity comes from the
contraction of space between masses. In this video, I explain my hypothesis and the work
needed to make it testable.

In the proposed picture, the space between two masses reaches its highest space/EM-energy
state: 100% oscillation and maximum expansion, normalized to 1. Space flows into both
masses. Although those flows point away from the middle, the intervening space contracts,
bringing the masses closer rather than pushing them apart.

I also explain the proposed 360° + 360° electron cycle, my infinite-path and least-action
interpretation of electron-to-electron energy transfer, and a speculative black-hole center
where oscillation slows, energy is stored as mass, and another incoming interaction
restarts space oscillation. Zero on the space/EM scale does not mean zero total energy.

This is a hypothesis under development. The animations illustrate the proposal; they are
not measurements of electron motion or a simulation proving the mechanism. The proposed
central concentration of galactic electrons is an assumption. The video separates
established physics from my postulates and discusses conservation laws, unresolved
light-speed formulas, and tests that could rule out a specified model.
