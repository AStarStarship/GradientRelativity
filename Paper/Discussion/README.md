# Discussion

The [current manuscript](../RoughDraftImproved.md) explains the clarified mechanism and
separates it from reference physics and illustrations.

## Speed of light invariance

The spatial 100% label denotes the highest proposed space/EM oscillation/expansion state
between masses. It does not demonstrate a varying local vacuum speed. Local vacuum speed,
coordinate speed, and effective-medium propagation are different quantities.

A new locally measured speed law needs a consistent field/spacetime model and comparisons
with reference physics. Calling every local value C(x) does not establish ordinary GR
local invariance or causality. P1 and the bounded prototype remain distinct, incompatible
candidate laws under the same internal fraction definition.

## Pressure, contraction, and distance

The proposed flows enter both masses from the intervening region. Condensation is proposed
to reduce the intervening space and hence the separation, rather than push the masses apart.
A completed model must connect pressure, conversion, matter response, and an operational
distance measured by declared observers. Coordinate compression or programmed motion does
not establish physical attraction.

## Conservation laws

For a closed unforced bookkeeping example, space and mass components can sum to a fixed
positive total. A driven system also requires incoming energy, outgoing energy, mechanical
work, recoil, and losses. Zero space/EM activity at the proposed center is not zero total
energy.

Momentum, angular momentum, and charge conservation must be derived from the field model;
labeling a gradient as their carrier does not prove conservation. In particular, the
space-dominant endpoint cannot silently eliminate electron charge. The stress-energy tensor
and source/receiver coupling have not yet been derived.

## Center and transport limitations

An infinite-path interpretation is not a sequential search through routes, and a finite
curve fan is not a path-integral evaluation. The proposed central majority-electron
population is an assumption rather than an observed census. Slowing, mass storage, and
incoming-hit restart need operational state and clock definitions and an accessible test.
The center is not the horizon, and restart does not establish an interior escape route.
