# Summary

Read the [current manuscript](../RoughDraftImproved.md) for the full argument and sources.

- Mass is proposed to be condensed space.
- The spatial 100% endpoint lies between masses and denotes the highest proposed space/EM
  oscillation/expansion state, normalized to 1. It is not a measured light-speed maximum.
- Pressure drives space into both masses in the proposed picture. The intervening space
  contracts, so their separation shrinks rather than the masses being pushed apart.
- The proposed internal electron cycle uses 360 degrees for space-to-mass conversion and
  another 360 degrees for return. Its illustrative interpolation does not derive spin.
- The transport proposal considers any electron as a possible source or receiver through
  infinitely many paths. Reference quantum amplitudes interfere; a finite path fan does not
  compute a functional integral or prove a least-action transport law.
- The proposed center has 0% space/EM activity, slow internal motion, and stored mass energy.
  A further incoming interaction restarts oscillation. Zero activity is not zero total energy.
- A central majority-electron population is an assumption, not an observed census.

The two speed-law candidates are incompatible under the same fraction definition. The
recorded galactic feedback budget is far below an electron rest energy per assumed orbit;
it does not test the separate incoming-photon driver. Conservation, electron structure,
pressure-driven distance changes, and predictions must still be derived and tested.
