# Core equations

The [current manuscript](../RoughDraftImproved.md)
separates reference relations, dimensional templates, and unresolved model choices.
Use SI unless a complete alternative unit convention is declared.

## Energy-momentum relation

The reference relation is

\[
E^2=(m c_0^2)^2+(p c_0)^2.
\]

At rest, \(E=m c_0^2\); for a massless photon, \(E=p c_0\). A new dispersion relation,
observable electron mass, or internal energy allocation must follow from the proposed
field dynamics. The spatial 100% label is not a measured speed or rest energy.

## Planck-scale relations

The reference expressions are

\[
l_P=\sqrt{\hbar G/c_0^3},\qquad E_P=\sqrt{\hbar c_0^5/G}.
\]

Consistent units with \(c_0=1\) do not make the Planck length zero. A variable-speed
substitution or Hawking-temperature relation is a further postulate, not an established
limit of these formulas.

For the declared internal fraction, two different candidate speed ratios are

\[
u_{\mathrm{P1}}^2=\frac{E_s}{E_m}=\frac{\sigma}{1-\sigma},
\qquad u_{\mathrm{bounded}}=2\sqrt{\sigma(1-\sigma)}.
\]

P1 diverges as \(\sigma\to1\), whereas the bounded form vanishes. Both give 1 at
\(\sigma=1/2\). This conflict is unresolved; neither candidate identifies the spatial
100% endpoint without a derived dictionary. The ratio of two energies is dimensionless,
so an SI speed squared cannot equal it without a reference speed factor.

## Driven harmonic oscillator

The dimensional template is

\[
m_e\ddot{x}+k_{\mathrm{eff}}x=F_{\mathrm{drive}}(t),
\qquad \omega_0=\sqrt{k_{\mathrm{eff}}/m_e}.
\]

An electromagnetic force uses \(qE(t)\), not charge times vacuum permittivity. The field,
damping, incoming coupling, and stored charged state remain unspecified. A constant-force
particular solution is an equilibrium displacement, not a sustained whirlpool.

The older galactic budget is a recorded classical approximation. It neither powers an
electron rest energy per assumed orbit nor determines the new photon-driven budget.
Charge, momentum, and driven energy conservation require field dynamics, not just two
fractions summing to one.
