# Abstract

Gradient Relativity proposes that mass is condensed space. The highest proposed space/EM
state lies between masses: 100% oscillation and maximum expansion, normalized to 1. Positive
pressure is proposed to drive space into both masses while the intervening space contracts,
reducing their separation. The proposal identifies this contraction as gravity rather than
repulsion. A separate 360-degree + 360-degree electron cycle exchanges space and mass
components. The transport interpretation considers infinitely many possible source-to-
receiver paths and a least-action outcome. At a speculative black-hole center, the space/EM
component approaches 0%, internal motion slows, energy is stored as mass, and another
incoming interaction restarts oscillation. Zero space/EM activity does not mean zero total
energy. The pressure law, conserved field dynamics, observable geometry, electron properties,
and quantitative tests remain unresolved; the animations illustrate the hypothesis.
