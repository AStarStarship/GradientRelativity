# Physical interpretations

The [current manuscript](../RoughDraftImproved.md) contains the sources, equations, and
limitations. The mechanisms below are postulates rather than measured interior dynamics.

## Electromagnetic waves and gravity

The proposed 100% space/EM endpoint between masses creates positive pressure driving space
into both masses. Space condenses into matter and the intervening space contracts. The
result is proposed as attraction through reduced separation, not a pair of repulsive forces.

A pressure/stress law and an operational distance calculation must establish this effect.
Galactic coordinate acceleration alone does not establish a radiating internal electron mode.

## Space-to-mass conversion

The internal two-turn cycle illustrates exchange between space and stored mass components.
A restart requires an incoming interaction and an energy budget. A component reaching zero
does not make total energy or electric charge vanish. The cycle does not yet derive spin,
observable mass, or a stable charged state.

## Black-hole dynamics

At the proposed center, internal motion slows and the space/EM component approaches 0%
while mass energy remains stored. A further hit restarts oscillation. The model needs a
clock, charged-state definition, coupling, losses, and causal response. These claims do not
describe an observed highest atomic level or an outgoing photon crossing a GR horizon.

The proposed central majority-electron population remains an assumption requiring a
specified epoch, composition, and an accessible quantitative consequence.

## Dark-matter hypothesis

The older interpretation of dark matter as non-emitting equilibrium electrons is not a
result of the current model. A proposed replacement needs galaxy-scale calculations,
composition constraints, and matched observable comparisons. None is supplied by the video
or the internal fraction plot.
