# Mathematical foundations

Definitions and sources are in the [current manuscript](../RoughDraftImproved.md), section 4.

## Special relativity review

Use the reference vacuum speed \(c_0\) in the baseline relation:

\[
E^2=(m c_0^2)^2+(p c_0)^2.
\]

The rest-frame and massless cases are \(E=m c_0^2\) and \(E=p c_0\), respectively. A modified
speed or dispersion relation must be derived; it cannot be introduced by replacing every
appearance of \(c_0\) with an unspecified spatial label.

## Gradient Relativity fundamentals

The normalized spatial space/EM label, internal electron-cycle fraction, and propagation
speed ratio are not yet connected by an action. For positive declared total \(E_0\), the
illustrative internal fraction gives

\[
E_s=\sigma E_0,\qquad E_m=(1-\sigma)E_0.
\]

The two speed candidates are \(u^2=\sigma/(1-\sigma)\) and
\(u=2\sqrt{\sigma(1-\sigma)}\). They share 1 at \(\sigma=1/2\) but have incompatible
\(\sigma\to1\) limits. The spatial 100% endpoint must not silently be identified with that
half-fraction point. Neither a unit convention nor a new name resolves the conflict.

## Hairy ball theory

The older notes invoke the Hairy Ball Theorem as a direction-selection argument. This
revision does not retain it as a derivation of electromagnetic radiation, electron spin,
or source-to-receiver transport. Its field domain, assumptions, and actual dynamical
consequences would have to be specified before using it in the physical model.
