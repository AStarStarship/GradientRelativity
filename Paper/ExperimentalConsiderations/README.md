# Experimental considerations

The [current manuscript](../RoughDraftImproved.md), section 7,
and [falsification roadmap](../Todo/electron_cycle_falsification.md) describe calculation
targets. The mechanism does not yet predict a numerical new effect.

## Photon nature

Specify the source spectrum, interacting electron or bound system, recoil partner,
cross-sections, receiving states, losses, and observation window. Separate scattering,
absorption, and capture. Compare the proposed photon-driven internal conversion with a
no-photon control and matched reference QED.

The recorded galactic classical budget is not the photon-driver budget. No universal
C-to-the-minus-four transparency or additional redshift factor is retained as a prediction
without a derivation that specifies the medium and constants held fixed.

## Electron measurement techniques

Derive a stable charged state, rest energy, spin, magnetic moment, scattering, and detector
response before treating a trap or spectrum as a test of the new mechanism. A 720-degree
trajectory does not derive these properties. A Compton reference frequency is not evidence
of an observed rotating interior.

A magnetic-moment comparison must include reference-theory uncertainty and uncertainty in
input constants. A null result excludes a specified predicted response, not every possible
hidden cycle.

## Conducting surface phenomena

Any proposed transport or current-decay signature needs a conserved source/receiver model,
geometry, material response, causal timescale, and matched electromagnetic baseline. The
older instantaneous-voltage and persistent-whirlpool interpretations are not established
by the current field template.

## Double-slit experiment insights

The source-to-receiver proposal must reproduce amplitudes, interference, and detector
probabilities for a declared setup. A finite fan of possible paths does not calculate a
functional integral. A fringe shift needs a specified gradient source, predicted magnitude,
and uncertainty; a drawing alone does not supply a scattering or decoherence theory.

## Pressure and center-restart tests

For two masses, derive a pressure/conversion law and an operational separation change
rather than impose contraction. Compare with the same geometry, boundary conditions,
physical time, and distance procedure in the reference model.

For restart, define the stopped internal variable, stored energy, incoming interaction,
charge continuity, recoil and losses. Compare hit/no-hit controls over the same time. If a
center extension yields no accessible observable, identify it as untestable. The central
electron population must be constrained rather than used as measured input.
