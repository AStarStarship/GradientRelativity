# Cosmological implications

The [current manuscript](../RoughDraftImproved.md) presents a local mechanism and its open
derivation targets. It does not yet supply a cosmological solution.

## Universal expansion and heat death

The spatial label 1 denotes the author's proposed maximum space/EM oscillation/expansion
between masses. It is not a Hubble expansion rate, a measured dark-energy density, or a
calculated heat-death history. Connecting it to cosmology requires a covariant action,
stress-energy tensor, declared spacetime solution, initial conditions, and observable tests.

## Big Bang and multiverse scenarios

The earlier rough draft discusses new-universe and multiverse interpretations. They are
historical speculative extensions, not consequences derived by the current pressure,
transport, or center-restart account. They are outside this manuscript's quantitative
claims until a model yields a defined test.
