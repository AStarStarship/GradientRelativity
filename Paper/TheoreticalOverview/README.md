# Theoretical overview

The [current manuscript](../RoughDraftImproved.md) separates reference physics, the author's
postulates, imposed animations, and recorded approximation results.

## Spatial mechanism

The highest proposed space/EM-energy state is between masses, at 100% oscillation/expansion
normalized to 1. Positive pressure drives space into both masses, where it condenses into
mass. The flows point away from the midpoint, but the intervening space contracts. The
proposal identifies the shrinking separation with gravity, not a repulsive force.

The pressure, conversion rate, matter response, and measured distance need a quantitative
law. Moving masses in an animation does not supply that law.

## Internal cycle and transport

The proposed electron cycle exchanges space and mass components over 360 degrees and a
further 360-degree return. An incoming interaction drives or restarts oscillation. The
internal phase is not yet linked to the spatial normalized label or measured light speed.

The author considers infinitely many possible paths between electron sources and receivers.
Reference amplitude sums and stationary action provide context, not a completed transport
kernel for the new mechanism.

## Center extension

The space/EM component is proposed to approach 0% at the black-hole center while mass energy
is stored. Internal motion slows; a further incoming hit restarts oscillation. A central
majority of electrons is an assumption, not a measured population. The center must not be
identified with the horizon, and restart does not establish photon escape from its interior.
