# Conclusion

The [current manuscript](../RoughDraftImproved.md) proposes mass as condensed
space, a 100% space/EM endpoint between masses, and pressure-driven flow into matter that
contracts the intervening space. Its internal two-turn cycle, possible-path transport, and
center storage/restart mechanism remain postulates.

The spatial zero labels the proposed space/EM component, not total energy. The central
majority-electron population is an assumption. A periodic plot does not derive spin, and
the two speed candidates are not equivalent under one fraction definition.

The next required work is a conserved quantitative field model, an operational distance
prediction, a photon-driver energy budget, electron observables, and matched tests against
reference physics. The animations explain the proposal; they do not validate it.
